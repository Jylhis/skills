# Inspiration galleries

Use these when the brief has no reference signals (section 0.A item 3) and the
vibe is unclear. Offer one gallery that fits the read, ask the user to pick one
or two sites they like, then design from those. Galleries are for choosing a
direction, not for copying a layout: take the principle (type scale, density,
motion restraint), never the composition wholesale.

| Gallery | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [hoverstat.es](https://hoverstat.es/) | "Awwwards / experimental / agency / kinetic type" reads | Free, near-daily updates, alternative and interaction-heavy sites |
| [httpster](https://httpster.net/) | Matching a vibe word to real sites | Free, ~3100 sites; style filters (minimal, typographic, brutalist, dark, colourful) and type filters |
| [unsection](https://unsection.com/) | One section at a time (hero, pricing, FAQ, testimonials, footer, navbar) | Free browsing; use when the page is right but one section is weak |
| [curated.design](https://curated.design/) | Industry-first lookup (SaaS, AI, dev tools, fintech, e-commerce, portfolio) | Free browsing; advanced filters are paid (Curated Pro) |

Out of scope here: product user-flow libraries (Page Flows, Mobbin) cover
multi-step product UI, which this skill does not handle.
