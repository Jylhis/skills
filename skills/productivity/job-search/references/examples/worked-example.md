# Worked example

A compressed end-to-end run, to show the expected output shape. The candidate
provides an existing resume and a posting for a "Senior Data Engineer" at a
fictional company, Acme Data.

## Phase 1 — extracted posting requirements (shown back to candidate)

**Must-have:** 5+ years data engineering; strong SQL; Python; a cloud warehouse
(Snowflake/BigQuery/Redshift); batch and streaming pipelines.
**Nice-to-have:** dbt; Airflow; cost-optimization experience; mentoring.
**Impact:** own the analytics pipeline that finance and product depend on.

**Mapping / question:** "Your history shows 6 years and strong SQL/Python/BigQuery
and Airflow, but I don't see streaming (Kafka/Kinesis). Do you have streaming
experience I should surface, or should we position batch depth as the strength?"

## Phase 3 — tailored experience bullet (before → after)

Before:
> Responsible for maintaining data pipelines and helping the analytics team.

After:
> Owned the batch analytics pipeline (BigQuery + Airflow) feeding finance and
> product dashboards; cut nightly runtime 45% and cloud cost ~$4k/month by
> repartitioning and pruning idle DAGs.

## Phase 4 — cover letter opening (specific hook)

> Acme Data's move to make its warehouse the single source of truth for finance
> and product is exactly the kind of ownership I want. For the last three years I
> have run the pipeline two teams depended on daily, and I would bring that same
> reliability focus to your analytics platform.
